Fall Library Challenge

Contest runs from Monday, August 24, 2026, until Friday, September 11, 2026.

Whether you’re on campus or online, join the Fall Library Challenge to uncover unique library spaces, services, and resources – and earn chances to win prizes!

The more challenges you complete, the more prize entries you will gain!

Find a library challenge station either online or in person at our Rutherford, Cameron, Sperber, Augustana and Bibliothèque Saint-Jean locations. Each in-person library challenge station has a QR code – scan it to fill out a form and submit your entry.

Prizes
  • 1 Grand Prize - Library VIP Prize:
    • $150 in ONEcard cash and priority access to study room booking for the Fall 2026 term and priority access to study room booking for the Fall 2026 term
  • 5 Additional Prizes:
    • $50 in ONEcard cash each (one for every Library location).
How to Participate
  • Current U of A students are eligible
  • Find a library challenge station (either online or in person)
  • Scan the QR code and answer the challenge question correctly.
